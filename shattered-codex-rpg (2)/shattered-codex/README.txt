THE SHATTERED CODEX — a first-person wizard RPG
================================================
Open index.html in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari). No install or server needed.
Progress autosaves every 30 seconds (browser local storage).

DESKTOP
  Click the game to capture the mouse.  WASD move · Mouse look · Space jump
  Left / Right click  cast hotbar slots 1 / 2 (hold to keep casting)
  1-0  cast hotbar spells       E  talk / open chest / pray at shrine
  Q / R  healing / mana potion  I  character & inventory   B  spellbook
  J  quest journal   M  map     Esc  pause & settings
  While flying: look to steer, Space rises, C descends.

MOBILE / TOUCH
  Drag on the left side to walk (up = forward).  Drag on the right side to look.
  Tap hotbar icons to cast (hold to repeat). Buttons for jump, potions, interact, menus.

TIPS
  * Villagers marked ! have quests; ? means ready to turn in. The main quest unlocks
    Blink, Town Portal, Water Walking, Invisibility, Fly, Beacon, Meteor and Starfall.
  * The world grows more dangerous the further you travel from Brightwater.
  * Each level gives 1 attribute point (2 every fifth level); each point is strong, so
    choose carefully (I). At certain levels you may learn a new spell: a pulsing
    "Learn spell" button appears - open it (or the spellbook, B) whenever you are out
    of danger, pick a spell and press Learn to confirm.
  * Elites (gold ring) and bosses (red ring) always drop good loot. Uniques change how
    spells work - build around them.
  * Combos: Frost Nova then Ice Lance (triple damage on frozen). Fireball/Firebolt then
    Combust. Hex makes enemies take 30% more damage. Toxic Cloud stacks poison.
  * Drag items between the grid and equipment slots; right-click to equip/use/sell.

WHAT'S NEW IN VERSION 2
  * Painted creature art for every monster, boss and villager, with walk and attack
    animation frames.
  * A new landscape: erosion-carved valleys, terraced desert mesas, volcanic cones with
    lava craters, burial mounds, lake basins and layered mountain rock.
  * Procedurally grown 3D trees (oak, birch, pine, willow, palm and more) that sway in
    the wind, wind-blown grass with wildflowers, mossy boulders, clouds, depth-shaded
    water with shoreline foam, and chimney smoke in the villages.
  * Mouse-look resumes automatically when you close a dialog, shop or menu.
  * Attribute points were rebalanced; saves from version 1 get their points refunded
    to spend again.
  * If the game runs slowly, set View distance to Near in the menu (Esc).
